```
SELECT *
FROM chat_records
WHERE (sender_id = @user_id1 AND receiver_id = @user_id2)
   OR (sender_id = @user_id2 AND receiver_id = @user_id1)
ORDER BY timestamp DESC
LIMIT 10
```
